YouTube cookies

YouTube sometimes asks the player to confirm it is not a bot, and then a track or a clip does not start. Cookies of a signed-in session remove that wall: the player asks YouTube as a normal listener. The cookies stay on this computer and are sent to YouTube only.
Important: the cookies must be of the YouTube website itself
  1. Pick your browser and install the extension from the link below.
  2. Open youtube.com in that browser and make sure you are signed in.
  3. While the YouTube tab is open, click the extension icon and press Export (or Copy) — it exports the cookies of the current site only. Cookies of Google search, Gmail or any other site will not work.
  4. Tip: sign in to YouTube in a private (incognito) window, export from it and close the window. In an ordinary tab YouTube keeps replacing the cookies, and the exported ones stop working («The page needs to be reloaded»).
  5. Paste what you got into the field below. The player checks it right away; then press Save.
Or take them from a browser by itself: the player reads the cookies from the browser you are signed in with. Close that browser before playing: it may lock its own cookie file.

Proxy / VPN

Use this when YouTube, SoundCloud or a clip does not open from your network. The choice applies to search, playback, clips and updates of the YouTube engine.
Connection
http://host:port, socks5://host:port or socks5://user:password@host:port
About VPN
  1. A VPN that works for the whole computer (WireGuard, OpenVPN, WARP and so on) needs no setup here: just turn it on, the player goes through it.
  2. A VPN or browser extension that sets a system proxy: choose “System proxy or VPN”.
  3. A VPN that opens a local proxy port (for example 127.0.0.1:1080 or :7890): choose “My own proxy” and enter that address.
