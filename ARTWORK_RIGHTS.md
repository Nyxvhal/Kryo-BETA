# Artwork rights


**Redistribution permissions for bundled third-party artwork are not verified. Do not publish a build with these assets until authorized, or replace/remove them.**

- VTuber portraits and seasonal models relate to characters, model authors, artists and/or their rightsholders. Public availability on YouTube, agency websites or model catalogs is not a redistribution license.
- Source/provenance records for 82 paired portraits are in `research/v12/artwork-manifest.json`; older collection notices are under `kryonyx/avatar-notices`. A source link is not proof of permission or authorship for derivatives.
- Sameko Saba and retained Ironmouse variants, including historical Lost Season 0, remain third-party likenesses. Season labels are cataloging, not an official collaboration claim.
- A character-free preview mode only hides portraits in previews. It does not remove assets from this archive.
- VTuber theme channel-avatar URLs, download hashes and explicit unresolved entries are recorded in `research/channel-avatars-v37.json`. YouTube channel avatars are not licensed by public availability; obtain redistribution permission before publishing. Four Ironmouse season palettes reuse the current channel avatar, not claimed historical channel icons.
- Names such as original, redesigned or signature do not establish a collection's license.
- Uploaded images remain subject to their actual owners' rights. The player cannot assign MIT, CC0 or another license to them.
- Distributors must establish the origin and rights for the application logo too; a code license alone does not do that.

Before public release, record file → rightsholder → source → legal basis → attribution/restrictions. Do not invent permissions. Without a valid basis, remove or replace the corresponding assets and catalog references.
